8. CONSOLIDATED FUTUREDRIVE PROMPT
----------------------------------

The following is a cleaned-up combined prompt based on the user's competition brief and project instructions. It is a consolidated version, not a verbatim message:

“Build a premium, futuristic automotive website called FutureDrive as a frontend-only prototype for a website competition. Use a professional black and dark-purple theme with violet neon accents, cinematic automotive imagery, modern typography, smooth animations, and responsive layouts.

Create a strong hero section with a large, sharp futuristic car image, a bold headline, a short description, and a clear Explore Vehicles action. Add a vehicle showcase featuring five fictional concept cars: Aether X, Vanta R, Nova S, Zenith E, and Orion GT. Include useful vehicle details, specifications, performance statistics, and attractive vehicle cards. Add technology, performance, and contact sections with clear navigation.

The design should feel premium, modern, polished, and visually impressive, inspired by futuristic automotive brands such as Tesla, BYD, Lamborghini, and Porsche, without copying their branding.

Keep the website frontend-only using HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Do not require a backend, database, authentication, payments, or real user data. Make it work locally by opening index.html in a browser; do not require Live Server.

A CSS-based 3D-inspired experience was explored, but the Digital Garage / 3D Showroom section was later requested to be removed. Keep the final website focused on the main automotive website and vehicle showcase. Ensure the hero image is high-resolution and sharp, not blurry. Include a short README.”
